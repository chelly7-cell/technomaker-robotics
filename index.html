Our Robots

Two robots. One platform.

01 / ALL-TERRAIN

All-Terrain Robot

Our All-Terrain robot is a compact and robust mobile platform designed for robotics competitions.

Equipped with four wheels, it provides good stability, traction, and mobility while remaining easy to maneuver.

Its goal is simple: move efficiently, stay stable, and adapt to different competition challenges.

4 Wheels Stable Mobile Competition
02 / ROCKET LEAGUE

Rocket League Robot

The Rocket League robot uses the same mobile platform as our All-Terrain robot.

An additional front mechanism allows the robot to interact with the ball during the competition.

The challenge is to move quickly around the field, maneuver effectively, control the ball, and push it toward the goal.

Fast Maneuverable Ball Control Competition
